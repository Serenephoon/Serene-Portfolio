MY STORY
Why I'd Chose to Commit
Interestingly, insurance was not my first career choice.
When I was first introduced to this industry, I had many doubts. I worried about whether I could succeed, whether I could earn a stable income, and even whether selling insurance might affect my relationships with friends.
But I decided to give myself a chance.
I started learning, meeting people, and building my business step by step. Along the way, I realised something important:
Insurance is not really about selling a policy. It is about being there when someone’s life does not go according to plan.
That understanding changed the way I looked at this career — and it is one of the reasons I have stayed for more than 20 years.
My ACHIVEMENTS
My Motivation
My journey was never about becoming successful overnight.
I started from zero, learning through rejection, mistakes, and experience. Every stage of my career taught me something different.

Over the years, I have achieved multiple MDRT, COT and TOT recognitions.
But what matters most to me is not the award itself.

Every recognition represents the trust of many clients, many conversations, many financial plans, and many years of consistency.

Awards recognise performance, but trust is the achievement I value most.
